Pricing Transparency

What's Included?

Clear guidelines on base inclusions and route-dependent charges to prevent surprises and avoid pricing disputes.

Standard Base Inclusions

  • Vehicle: Clean, well-maintained vehicle in the booked category.
  • Driver: Experienced, verified commercial highway chauffeur.
  • Fuel: Included for agreed route kilometers and itinerary.
  • Route-based travel arrangements: Coordinated pickups and drop-offs.
  • Customer support: Dedicated coordination assistance during travel.

Route & Quotation Dependent Items

Tolls & Highway Taxes: Fastag charges vary by highway corridor.
State Border Permits: Commercial entry taxes when crossing state lines.
Parking Charges: Settled based on actual receipt where applicable.
Driver Allowance: Applicable for overnight and multi-day travel.
All applicable inclusions and charges will be communicated in the quotation before confirmation.
